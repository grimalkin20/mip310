Course Overview

About M.Pharm

The Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm) is a 2-year postgraduate program that provides advanced knowledge and specialized training in various branches of pharmaceutical sciences. This program is designed for students who want to pursue careers in research, academia, or high-level positions in the pharmaceutical industry.

Program Highlights

  • Advanced specialization in chosen pharmaceutical discipline
  • Extensive research work and dissertation project
  • State-of-the-art research laboratories and equipment
  • Faculty with Ph.D. and industry experience
  • Industry collaborations and research partnerships
  • Publication opportunities in peer-reviewed journals
  • Gateway to Ph.D. and academic careers

Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the M.Pharm program, graduates will be able to:

  • Conduct independent research in pharmaceutical sciences
  • Design and develop innovative pharmaceutical products
  • Apply advanced analytical techniques and instrumentation
  • Lead pharmaceutical research and development teams
  • Teach pharmaceutical sciences at undergraduate level
  • Contribute to regulatory science and drug approval processes
  • Pursue Ph.D. and advanced research careers

Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements

  • Educational Qualification: B.Pharm degree from a recognized university
  • Minimum Marks: 55% aggregate in B.Pharm (50% for reserved categories)
  • Entrance Exam: GPAT/NIPER JEE/State Level entrance exam
  • Age Limit: No upper age limit
  • Work Experience: Preferred but not mandatory
Entrance Examinations
GPAT - Graduate Pharmacy Aptitude Test
NIPER JEE - Joint Entrance Examination
State Level - State entrance examinations
Institutional - College specific entrance test
