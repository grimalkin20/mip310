About B.Pharm

The Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) is a 4-year undergraduate program that provides comprehensive knowledge in pharmaceutical sciences, drug development, and clinical pharmacy. This program is designed to prepare students for diverse career opportunities in the pharmaceutical industry, healthcare sector, and research organizations.

Program Highlights

  • Comprehensive undergraduate curriculum covering all aspects of pharmacy
  • Industry-oriented training with modern laboratory facilities
  • Industrial visits and internships with pharmaceutical companies
  • Research projects and dissertation work
  • Clinical pharmacy training in hospitals
  • Professional development and soft skills training

Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the B.Pharm program, graduates will be able to:

  • Understand pharmaceutical sciences and drug development processes
  • Design and develop various pharmaceutical dosage forms
  • Conduct quality control and assurance of pharmaceutical products
  • Provide clinical pharmacy services and patient counseling
  • Work in pharmaceutical manufacturing and regulatory affairs
  • Pursue advanced studies in pharmacy and related fields
  • Engage in pharmaceutical research and development

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum Marks: 50% aggregate in PCB/PCM (45% for reserved categories)
  • Age Limit: No upper age limit
  • Entrance Exam: NEET/JEE/State Level entrance exam or Merit based
  • Domicile: No domicile restrictions
