About
Magadh Institute of Pharmacy

Established with a vision to provide quality education in the field of pharmacy, Magadh Institute of Pharmacy has been a beacon of excellence in pharmaceutical sciences.

Our institution is committed to nurturing competent pharmacy professionals who contribute significantly to the healthcare sector. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and innovative teaching methodologies, we prepare our students for successful careers in pharmacy.

Approved by Pharmacy Council of India
Approved by Bihar University of Health Sciences (BUHS) &
Aryabhatta Knowledge University (AKU)
Also Recognised by Health Department Govt. of Bihar

Admission Process

Eligibility Criteria

D.Pharm

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics
  • Minimum 50% marks
  • Age: 17-23 years

B.Pharm

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics
  • Minimum 50% marks
  • Valid entrance exam score

M.Pharm

  • B.Pharm from P.C.I recognized university
  • Minimum 55% marks
  • GPAT/NIPER JEE qualified
